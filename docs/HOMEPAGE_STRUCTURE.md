## 📐 Layout & Design Principles

### **Color Scheme** (from design-tokens.ts):
- **Primary**: Navy Blue (text-blue-900) - Trust & Authority
- **Accent**: Gold/Amber (text-amber-600) - Premium & Luxury
- **Neutral**: Gray (text-gray-700) - Secondary text
- **Background**: White (default), Light Gray (alternating sections)
- **Dark**: Navy Blue (footer, dark CTAs)

### **Typography**:
- **Font Family**: Montserrat (all text)
- **Headings**: Bold, Navy Blue
- **Body**: Regular, Gray
- **Accents**: Gold for emphasis

### **Spacing & Layout**:
- **Section Gaps**: 8px (mobile) → 12px → 16px (desktop)
- **Container Padding**: 4px (mobile) → 6px → 8px (desktop)
- **Responsive Grid**: 1 col (mobile), 2 col (tablet), 3 col (desktop)

### **Responsive Strategy**:
- Mobile-first approach
- Tailwind breakpoints: sm, md, lg
- Test on: iPhone, iPad, Desktop (1024px+)
- All text must be readable
- Touch-friendly buttons (min 44px)

### **Accessibility**:
- ✅ Navy on White (contrast ≥ 4.5:1)
- ✅ Semantic HTML (header, section, nav, footer)
- ✅ Proper heading hierarchy (h1, h2, h3)
- ✅ Alt text for all images
- ✅ WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
